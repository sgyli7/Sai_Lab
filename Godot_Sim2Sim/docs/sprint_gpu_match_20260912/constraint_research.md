# Why constraint learning is the next controlled experiment

The first GPU candidates increase native long speed from0.2252 to0.2983/0.2873m/s, but pass51/128 and53/128 against S05's120/128. The dominant regression is turning, with additional stopping failures. This is visible in GPU too: on actual native initial poses/tapes, S05 with game feet passes129/129; learned exact-feet actor66/129, allzero falls. Geometry matching alone does not resolve an objective trade-off.

The present reward pays6 times forward speed but charges only0.5 times squared yaw-rate error. Going faster can outweigh a substantial steering loss; this follows directly from scripts/sprint_gpu_train.py, not a claim inferred from papers.

[CaT, IROS2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18765v1) weights current rewards and future returns by a survival probability based on constraint violations, without using that learning termination to reset physical trajectories. Its authors report experiments on a real Solo quadruped. Here we implement this return mechanism with project-specific local surrogates for the existing turn/path/heading/stop criteria. It is not a formal safety guarantee and does not replace native acceptance.

[SII2026 humanoid CaT](https://humanoid-cat.github.io/) extends this approach to humanoid locomotion and simulated package transport; this is relevant recent architectural evidence, not proof of our MicroDuck transfer or commercial deployment. [CoRL2025 non-conflicting energy optimization](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v305/peri25a.html) separately demonstrates gradient projection to reduce competition between objectives, including GO2 sim-to-real experiments; speed/control priority is our proposed analogy, not that paper's tested task.

Controlled extension: freeze the exact-foot GPU domain, sourceS05, same128x64x512 budget/seed951101 and ordinaryS05. Compare nonnegative reward control with zero constraint probability against identical nonnegative reward plus CaT probability. No new game controller, physics, actor inputs, final seeds, or relaxed acceptance. Four violations are turning below60% command after1s, lateral path outside max(.05m,10%forward), heading beyond15deg, and0.2s mean speed above.05m/s after2s idle. These are per-step training surrogates, not an exact reproduction of complete trajectory scoring. CaT EMA.95 and probability.05→.25 follow a predeclared128iteration schedule. All candidates are fixed training endpoints; no checkpoint selection.
